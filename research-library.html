About This Library

Methodology & Data Sources

These guides and charts represent two independent forecasting approaches applied to the same Columbus GBFS fleet data. Both use historical event catalogs, ISO-week correlations, and weather signals to project demand. The side-by-side comparison enables validation and cross-methodological review.

Data lineage: All forecasts derive from public GBFS feeds (Spin and Veo), publicly available holiday/event calendars, and historical weather records. No proprietary data. Full methodologies documented in the Word guides.
